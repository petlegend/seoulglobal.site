About us

At the forefront of Korea–Russia business

Seoul Global Co., Ltd. is the lead company of Seoul Global Group. It manages the group's strategy and investments and provides advisory and agency services to Korean companies that want to reopen trade with Russia or enter the Russian market for the first time. Pet Legend Co., Ltd. handles trade in Korea and DOMOKUP LLC runs operations in Moscow, so what we advise, the group carries out itself.
